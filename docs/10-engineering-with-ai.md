# Module 10: Engineering with AI

:::warning Not yet published
This module is under construction.
:::

You met Claude in Module 1 as a chat window with two rules. This module
is the deeper end, and you've earned it: agentic tools that work in your
terminal and repos rather than a browser tab, packaging your own
repeatable procedures as skills, turning shell history and lab sessions
into documentation that sounds like you, and the review discipline that
makes delegation safe.

It sits here, after nine modules of manual work and one of automation,
on purpose. You can only safely delegate what you can verify, and the
verifier in that sentence is you. The modules behind you built that
verifier. Everything past this point (detection, vulnerability
management, the attack modules) will let you put an AI to work the way
practicing engineers do, because now you can check its work.
