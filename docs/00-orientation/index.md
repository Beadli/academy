# Module 0: Orientation

Somewhere out there is a junior admin sweating through their first week
because a hiring manager assumed "knows computers" meant "has seen Group
Policy before." I built this course for that person, a few years earlier.

By the end of the full curriculum you will have built a small mock
enterprise on your own hardware: Active Directory, a real certificate
authority, single sign-on, Docker services, Ansible automation, and a SIEM
watching all of it. Then you'll attack it from Kali and investigate your own
alerts. Every piece of software involved is free. The price is your time.

This module needs no hardware and no installs. It's a sit-down-and-read
module, and it exists because starting a lab without orientation is how
people end up with four half-built VMs and no idea why any of them matter.

What's in it:

- **0.1** what an enterprise environment looks like from the inside
- **0.2** the people who run it, and which of their chairs you'll sit in
- **0.3** the lab you're going to build, and what hardware it honestly needs
- **0.4** how this course works: numbering, checkpoints, journals, AI
- **0.5** your first journal entry
- **0.6** the checkpoint: measure your machine and pick your tier

One evening is enough. Don't skip 0.6; every later module assumes you did
it.
